Arrowords ("we," "our," or "us") provides a personalized word puzzle experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data. Because our app is designed for users of all ages, including children, we have implemented high standards of data protection.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account (no name, email, or password required). To provide the core functionality of the app, we collect:

  • Persistent Identifiers: A unique Client ID is generated on your device and stored in Local Storage. This ID is used solely for internal operations, such as maintaining your puzzle history and preferences.
  • User Content: We store the list of words you wish to learn, words you have reported as inappropriate, and a history of the puzzles generated for you.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for the following:

  • Puzzle Generation: We send your Client ID and your "words to learn" to our secure AWS (Amazon Web Services) servers to run our custom generation algorithm.
  • Filtering: We use your "reported words" list to ensure your puzzles remain personalized, relevant, and safe.
  • App Functionality: To maintain your progress and saved puzzles.

3. Service Providers

We use AWS (Amazon Web Services) to host our puzzle generation logic and store user-generated content. AWS is a secure cloud provider. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or any other third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Arrowords is intended for a general audience, including children. We comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do not collect names, contact information, or precise location data from children.
  • The Client ID is used solely to support the internal operations of the app (personalizing the app experience), which is an authorized use under COPPA.
  • If you are a parent and wish to request the deletion of data associated with your child's device, please contact us at the email provided above.

5. Data Security & Retention

We use HTTPS encryption to protect your data while it is being transmitted to our AWS servers. We retain your word lists and puzzle history as long as the app is installed. You may request data deletion at any time by contacting the developer via the "Data Deletion Request" section above.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
